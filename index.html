How it works

From first probe to first call in ten days.

No trenching, no wiring, no data science team. A crew of two can cover a 50 hectare farm before lunch.

Velvet Ridge · 48 ha
Block C-4 · Irrigate 11 mm · Thu 05:30Sent to pump controller P-2 and the field team
Step 1

Plant the probes

Push a G3 probe into the root zone with the T-handle. Six minutes each. One probe covers about two hectares of uniform soil.

12 sensors per probe5-year batteryIP68
Step 2

The mesh finds itself

Probes discover each other over LoRa and route around dead spots. One solar gateway covers up to 800 hectares, even with no cell signal in the field.

2 km hop rangeStore and forward
Step 3

The model learns your soil

For ten days Mycora watches how water moves through each layer and fits a hydraulic profile to every block. It keeps learning every season.

Field capacityRefill pointDrainage rate
Step 4

You get one clear call

Every morning: which blocks to water, how many millimetres, and when. Send it to your pump controller or to your crew's phones by SMS.
